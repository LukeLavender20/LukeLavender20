# Conditional Access, MFA, And Passwordless Rollout

Identity policy should reduce risk without locking normal employees out of the tools they need.

## Rollout Stages

1. Inventory users, admins, guests, service accounts, and break-glass accounts.
2. Confirm authentication methods registration.
3. Create report-only Conditional Access policies.
4. Review sign-in logs and failure patterns.
5. Pilot enforcement with a controlled group.
6. Expand enforcement after help desk paths are ready.
7. Monitor registration gaps and risky sign-ins.

## Policy Categories

- Require MFA for admins.
- Require MFA for user risk or unfamiliar sign-in patterns.
- Block legacy authentication.
- Require compliant or hybrid joined device for sensitive apps when ready.
- Exclude break-glass accounts with separate monitoring.
- Use report-only mode before enforcement for broad policies.

## Evidence To Keep

- Policy name and mode.
- Included and excluded users/groups.
- Apps covered.
- Grant controls.
- Session controls.
- Sign-in log sample.
- Help desk rollback path.
